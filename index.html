Scoring rubric & methodology

Scores are subjective human judgments, not objective truths. Weights are transparent and adjustable in code (js/scoring.js).

Weighted score

Weighted score per response = Σ(criterion score × weight) / Σ(weight). Weights: factual accuracy 0.25, relevance 0.20, Swahili fluency 0.20, instruction following 0.15, cultural appropriateness 0.10, safety 0.10. A response with any missing rating is marked incomplete and excluded from averages.

Verdict logic

Evaluators may choose A, B, both, or neither. The dashboard does not force a verdict from scores; it records the human choice and displays it alongside weighted scores for transparency.

Limitations

  • All data is synthetic and demonstration-only.
  • Swahili fluency judgments are inherently subjective and vary by region and register.
  • Small samples in localStorage are not statistically meaningful.
  • Ambiguous tasks are explicitly labeled and should be treated cautiously.
